gws-cli · Personal Google tools

Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026. This policy describes the personal Google account integration operated by Fernando H-T Goldaraz using gws, gogcli, and selected AI assistants.

Purpose and data access

The integration is intended for the operator's accounts and personally invited account holders who authorize access. Each account holder grants access through Google's consent screen. Depending on the granted permissions and requested task, it accesses account identity, email, calendar entries, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, tasks, and related Google API resources. Granted permissions can include modifying data; operations are performed for tasks requested by the account holder or a person they have authorized to assist them.

Processing and sharing

Command-line tools retrieve Google data in the operator's computing environment. The operator and authorized assistants can process the data needed for an authorized task. When an AI assistant is used, content selected for that task may be sent to and processed by that assistant's service provider. That provider's applicable account settings, privacy policy, and retention terms govern its processing. This integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are kept in the operator's local authentication storage, including the operating system keyring or encrypted credential files used by the tools. Invited accounts have separate credential storage. Commands for those accounts explicitly select the person and verify the account identity before running. Retrieved content may remain in downloaded files, tool caches, or assistant conversations according to the selected workflow. There is no separate public application database for Google user data.

Control, retention, and deletion

Each account holder can revoke the integration's access in their Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete existing local files or assistant conversations. Account holders can ask the operator to remove their local credentials and retained copies; assistant conversations and provider-held copies must be removed in the environment or provider where they are stored. There is no single retention period across these independent tools and providers.

This public website

These static information pages do not ask visitors to sign in and do not receive Google data or OAuth credentials. They contain no analytics scripts, forms, or advertising. GitHub Pages provides hosting; its infrastructure processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

The operator can be reached through his GitHub profile or the direct contact used for the personal invitation. This page will be updated if the integration's purpose or data handling changes.
