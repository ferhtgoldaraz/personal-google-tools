Personal account integration

gws-cli

Google account tools for private, personal work.

This OAuth application is operated by Fernando H-T Goldaraz for his own accounts and personally invited account holders who authorize access. It uses gws, gogcli, and selected AI assistants.

With the account holder's consent, it accesses Gmail, Calendar, Drive, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, tasks, and related Google APIs to carry out authorized tasks. Invited accounts use separate credential storage and explicit account selection. It is not a public service and has no public sign-up.

When an assistant is asked to work with Google data, the selected content may be processed by the assistant's environment and AI provider. The privacy policy explains this data flow, storage, and access controls.
